Our Story

Cuts, color & a warm welcome

U-Neek Cutz is a hair salon at 3450 Sharon Road in West Middlesex, Pennsylvania. Stylist Monica offers cut & coloring, and the salon is hiring licensed Pennsylvania stylists to join the team. A small, community-focused salon — it runs a Toys for Tots holiday toy drive, with collections until December 3rd, and thanks clients who keep the stylist in their budget.
