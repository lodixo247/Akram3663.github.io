Our Story

A woman's touch for a clean home

Cleaning Gals LLC is a cleaning service in Rapid City, South Dakota, serving the Black Hills and surrounding areas. Its tagline is “A WOMAN'S TOUCH FOR A CLEAN HOME” and its slogan is “CLEAN SPACES. STRONG IMPRESSIONS. LASTING PARTNERSHIPS.” The team is fully insured — General Liability and Workers' Compensation — and handles post-construction cleaning, final cleans before project turnover, commercial janitorial, move-in/move-out, common-area cleaning, office cleaning, window cleaning, floor cleaning and detailing, high dusting and final touch-ups.
